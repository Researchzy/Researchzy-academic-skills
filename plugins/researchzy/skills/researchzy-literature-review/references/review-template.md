# Review template

Use this structure for `literature-review/review.md`. Drop a section a quick map does not need; do not add sections for their own sake. Cite with the ledger's keys as `[@key]`.

```markdown
# <Topic>: a literature review

<date> · <N> papers included, <M> read in full · search log: search-log.md

## Questions

1. <question 1>
2. <question 2>

Scope: <period, fields, what is out>. Included: <criteria in one sentence>.

## Short answers

1. <the answer to question 1 in two or three sentences, with its strongest citations>
2. <…>

## How the papers were found

<The flow sentence from `review_ledger.py status`.> Searches ran in Researchzy's index in these
passes: <passes>. <One sentence on the library and seed papers.> The newest paper seen is from
<month year>.

## <Theme 1>

<What the papers of this theme establish together. Comparisons inside sentences. Disagreements
and the conditions that may explain them. What stays open.>

## <Theme 2>

…

## Comparison

| Paper | Design or method | Data | Main result | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [@key] | … | … | … | … |

Table: <what the table shows, as a statement>.

## Gaps

| Gap | How sure | Searches that looked for it | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| … | high / medium / low | s4, s9 | … |

## Limitations of this review

- <index coverage and newest date>
- <results looked at out of those reported; no forward citation search>
- <reading depth: full text, abstract only>

## Papers to read first

1. [@key] — <one line on why>, <reader link>
2. …
```

For a quick map keep: Questions, Short answers, How the papers were found (two sentences), Comparison, Gaps (if any), Limitations (two lines), Papers to read first.
