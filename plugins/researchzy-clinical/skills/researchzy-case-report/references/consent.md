# Consent for publication

The consent statement in the draft is generated by `case_check.py check` from `case-report/consent.json`, which records what the clinician's file says. It is never a stock sentence: the draft says that consent was obtained only when the record says so, and only what the record says.

## consent.json

```json
{"status": "obtained", "given_by": "patient", "relationship": "", "scope": ["text", "images"],
 "form": "written", "source": "signed form in the clinic file; copy held by the corresponding author",
 "recorded_by": "treating dermatologist", "language": "en"}
```

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| `status` | `obtained`, `waived`, `not_obtained`, `unknown` |
| `given_by` | `patient`, `parent_or_guardian`, `next_of_kin`, `legal_representative` (for `obtained`) |
| `relationship` | Optional, in the language of the report: "daughter", "女儿". Used for anyone but the patient |
| `scope` | A list of `text`, `images`, `video`: what the consent covers |
| `form` | `written`, `electronic`, `verbal_documented` (given verbally and recorded in the medical record) |
| `source` | Where the record is kept, never its content. Required for `obtained` and `waived` |
| `waived_by`, `reason` | Who waived the requirement, and why (`waived`); why there is no consent (`not_obtained`) |
| `language` | `en` or `zh`: the language of the generated statement |
| `recorded_by` | Who confirmed the record, by role |

A missing `consent.json` is read as status `unknown`.

## The generated statement

| Record | Statement |
|---|---|
| obtained; patient; text and images; written | Written informed consent for publication of this case report and the accompanying images was obtained from the patient. |
| obtained; next of kin, daughter; text; verbal, recorded | Verbal informed consent for publication of this case report was obtained from the patient's daughter and recorded in the medical record. |
| obtained; parent or guardian; text, images and video; written; zh | 已获得患者的父母或监护人的书面知情同意，同意发表本病例报告及所附图片和视频。 |
| waived | The requirement to obtain consent for publication was waived by {waived_by} ({reason}). |
| not obtained | Consent for publication was not obtained ({reason}). |
| unknown | [Author input needed: consent for publication] |

Copy the statement from `check.md` into the draft's consent section word for word, and write the free-text fields (`relationship`, `waived_by`, `reason`) so that they read well inside it. `check` fails:

- `consent_record`: a field missing or outside its vocabulary; status `unknown` in a `--final` run.
- `consent_statement`: the generated statement is not in the draft, or the draft says consent was obtained while the record says otherwise.
- `consent_scope`: the report has figures and `scope` does not include `images`.

If a free-text field holds an identifier, the statement is withheld from `check.md`; reword the field.

## Who gives consent

The clinician's record, the journal's instructions and the ethics rules the clinician works under decide what is acceptable. This skill records and words it; it does not judge it.

- A child: usually a parent or guardian, with the child's own agreement where the rules ask for it.
- A patient who has died: usually the next of kin; record the relationship.
- A patient who cannot decide: a legal representative, as the local rules define one.
- When no consent can be obtained and someone waived the requirement, the record names who did and why. Whether the journal accepts that is for its instructions to say: tell the user to check them.

## Rules

- Never write that consent was obtained unless `consent.json` says `obtained`, from the clinician's record.
- Never copy the signed form, or anything that bears the patient's name or signature, into `case-report/`. `source` says where the form is.
- An image needs consent that covers images. When `scope` lacks `images`, ask the user before including any figure.
- For `not_obtained`, tell the user plainly that the journal's instructions decide whether such a report can be submitted, and that this skill does not decide it.
